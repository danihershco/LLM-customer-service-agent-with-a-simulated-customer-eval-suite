# Maison Verre Returns & Exchanges Policy

Modeled on a real luxury retailer's public policy, simplified for this project.

## Return windows (counted from the delivery date)
- **Full-price items:** refund to the original payment method within 14 days of delivery. From day 15 to day 30, returns are accepted for site credit only. After day 30, items cannot be returned.
- **Markdown items:** returnable within 14 days of delivery, refunded to the original payment method. After day 14, markdown items cannot be returned. No site credit option.
- **Fine jewelry:** returnable within 14 days of delivery unless marked final sale.

## Final sale (no returns, refunds, or exchanges)
Any item marked final sale, including lingerie, fragrance and beauty, exotic leathers, vintage, custom, and special-order items.

## Condition
- Items must be new and unworn, with all original tags and packaging (boxes, dust bags, authenticity cards).
- Worn, washed, altered, or damaged-by-customer items cannot be returned.
- Shoes must be returned in the original shoe box with unmarked soles.

## Refunds
- Refunds go only to the original payment method on the order (or site credit where stated above). We cannot refund to a different card or account.
- Outbound shipping charges are not refunded.
- Refunds are processed within 8 to 10 business days after the return is received and inspected.
- If the original card has been cancelled or replaced, the refund is still issued to it: the customer's bank typically forwards it to the replacement card or account. If it doesn't arrive, the customer's bank can trace it. A cancelled card is not a reason to delay a return.

## Exchanges
- Within the refund window, a full-price or markdown item can be exchanged for a different size of the same product if that size is in stock.
- If the requested size is out of stock, offer a return instead.

## Gifts
- Only the person who placed the order can start a return or exchange. Gift recipients should ask the gift giver to contact us.

## Escalate to the client care team when
- An item arrived damaged or defective (escalate right away).
- The customer asks to speak with a person (escalate right away).
- The customer asks for an exception to this policy and still wants it reviewed after you've explained what a review can and can't change.
